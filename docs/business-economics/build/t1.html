01 · Basic idea

Economics kya study karta hai?

Core idea: Wants unlimited hain, resources (land, labour, capital, time, money) limited hain, aur har resource ke alternative uses hain. Isliye choice karni padti hai — aur har choice ki ek opportunity cost hoti hai.

Ek resource ek kaam mein lag gaya to wahi resource usi waqt doosre kaam mein nahi lag sakta. Isi conflict ko economic problem kehte hain.

Example: Town ke paas ₹10 lakh hain. Library sudhaare ya water supply? Dono useful, paisa ek ke liye hi kaafi. Jo chhoda, wahi opportunity cost.

03 · Definitions

Paanch definitions — meaning, features, criticism

1. Wealth definition — Adam Smith (1776)

"Economics is an inquiry into the nature and causes of the wealth of nations." (Smith ki book ke title se liya gaya common form)

Isi approach ke supporters: J. B. Say — "Economics is the science which treats of wealth", J. S. Mill, Walker.

  • Focus: wealth ka production, consumption, exchange, distribution.
  • Man ko "Economic Man" maana — sirf self-interest aur material gain se chalne wala.

Criticism: Wealth ko end aur man ko secondary bana diya · wealth ka narrow (sirf material) meaning · Economic Man unrealistic. Isi wajah se critics ne economics ko "dismal science" kaha (Carlyle).

2. Material welfare definition — Alfred Marshall (1890)

"Political Economy or Economics is a study of mankind in the ordinary business of life; it examines that part of individual and social action which is most closely connected with the attainment and with the use of the material requisites of well-being."

Supporters: A. C. Pigou, Edwin Cannan, William Beveridge.

  • Focus wealth se man par shift — wealth sirf means, welfare end.
  • "Ordinary business of life" — normal earning aur spending activities.
  • Sirf material welfare (goods jo touch/measure ho sakein).
  • Economics ek social science hai — society mein rehne wale real men ka study.

Criticism (mainly Robbins): Material vs non-material ka distinction galat — teacher, doctor ki services bhi welfare deti hain · welfare ko measure nahi kar sakte (value judgement) · kuch economic activities welfare nahi badhaati (jaise liquor) · definition classificatory hai, analytical nahi.

3. Scarcity definition — Lionel Robbins (1932)

"Economics is the science which studies human behaviour as a relationship between ends and scarce means which have alternative uses."
  • Unlimited ends — wants anant hain.
  • Scarce means — resources wants ke comparison mein kam.
  • Alternative uses — ek resource kai kaam aa sakta hai.
  • Ends differ in importance — isliye priority/choice possible.
  • Economics ek positive science hai aur "neutral between ends" — economist ends ko morally good/bad rank nahi karta.

Criticism: Static hai — growth aur time ko ignore karta hai · Keynes ka point: resources idle/unemployed bhi rehte hain, scarcity unemployment explain nahi karti · economist ka complete neutral rehna practical nahi · macro problems par kam focus.

Trap: Robbins ki definition "money-only" nahi hai. Scarcity–choice bina paise ke bhi apply hoti hai.

4. Growth definition — Paul A. Samuelson (1948)

"Economics is the study of how men and society end up choosing, with or without the use of money, to employ scarce productive resources which could have alternative uses, to produce various commodities over time and distribute them for consumption, now and in the future, among various persons and groups in society."
  • Robbins ka scarcity + alternative uses retain kiya.
  • Time dimension add kiya — "over time", "now and in the future" → dynamic, growth-oriented.
  • "With or without money" — barter economy bhi cover.
  • Production ke saath distribution among persons and groups.

Exam clue: "now and in the future", "over time", "with or without money" → Samuelson.

5. Wantlessness definition — Prof. J. K. Mehta

"Economics is a science which studies human behaviour as a means to the end of wantlessness."
  • Indian (Allahabad School) philosophical approach.
  • Wants badhaana nahi, wants kam karke wantlessness (complete satisfaction) tak pahunchna goal.

Trap: Robbins = unlimited wants ko maan kar choice; Mehta = wants ko hi kam karna. Dono ulte hain.

05 · Scarcity

Scarcity, shortage, free goods

{{FLOW_SVG}}

Scarcity

Resources wants ke relative kam hain. Ye permanent, universal problem hai — ameer desh mein bhi.

Shortage

Kisi particular price/time par quantity demanded > quantity supplied. Temporary market situation.

Free goods

Demand ke comparison mein abundant, zero price, koi opportunity cost nahi. Jaise khule mein hawa, dhoop.

Economic goods

Scarce hain, isliye price ya opportunity cost hai. Jaise anaaj, kapda, doctor ki service.

Trap: Free/economic hona context par depend karta hai. Khuli hawa free good, lekin submarine ya hospital ka oxygen cylinder economic good.

06 · Choice

Opportunity cost vs sunk cost

Opportunity cost = choice karne par chhoda gaya next best alternative ka value/benefit.
Sunk cost = pehle ho chuka, non-refundable cost jo aaj ke decision se wapas nahi aa sakta — isliye aaj ke decision mein ignore hota hai.

Example: 2 ghante padhai ki, isliye ₹400 ki paid shift chhoot gayi (best alternative). Opportunity cost = ₹400. Pichhle hafte li gayi non-refundable book ka price = sunk cost.

Trap: Opportunity cost sirf next best alternative ka hota hai, saare chhode gaye options ka total nahi.

07 · Central problems

Teen central problems of an economy

{{PROBLEMS_SVG}}
  • What to produce? — kaun se goods, kitni quantity (output mix, resource allocation).
  • How to produce? — kaunsi technique: labour-intensive ya capital-intensive.
  • For whom to produce? — output/income logon mein kaise bategi (distribution).

Kuch books do aur problems jodti hain: resources ka full & efficient use aur economic growth (resources badhana).

Trap: "What" = allocation (output mix); "For whom" = distribution. Dono alag hain.

Simple economy: household aur firm

Households consumer hain aur land, labour, capital ki services dete hain → badle mein rent, wages, interest (factor income). Firms in inputs se goods/services banati hain aur bechkar revenue kamati hain.

10 · Diagram

Production Possibility Curve (PPC)

PPC = given resources aur technology se do goods ke maximum possible combinations dikhane wala curve. Ise PPF / transformation curve bhi kehte hain.
  • Assumptions: sirf 2 goods · resources fixed · technology fixed · resources fully & efficiently used · resources har good mein equally efficient nahi.

11 · Numerical

Marginal Rate of Transformation (MRT)

MRT = X ki ek extra unit ke liye chhodi gayi Y ki units = marginal opportunity cost = PPC ki slope (absolute value).
{{MRT_SVG}}

ΔY = Y mein change, ΔX = X mein change. Absolute value isliye kyunki sacrifice positive number mein bataate hain (ΔY negative hota hai).

Worked example: X: 40 → 50 (ΔX = +10), Y: 80 → 60 (ΔY = −20)
MRT = |−20 / 10| = 2 → 1 extra X ki cost = 2 units Y.
Agle 10 X ke liye 30 Y chhodni pade to MRT = 3 → increasing MOC → concave PPC.
